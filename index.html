01

It reads everything

Every night Wraith crawls the career pages of 38 Kenyan banks — plus the applicant systems behind them: Workday, Oracle HCM, SmartRecruiters and others. Landing pages get crawled a level deeper, so listings buried behind “Career Opportunities” links are found too.

02

It knows what a job is

Bank websites are noisy — board profiles, internet-banking portals, press releases, product pages. Wraith’s classifier scores every link against dozens of signals and hard-vetoes the noise. What survives is an actual opening with an actual title.

03

It delivers, then disappears

One message, every morning at 07:00, in a private WhatsApp group. New postings only — deduplicated against everything already sent. No app to install, no inbox to manage, nothing to check twice.
