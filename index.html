💼 How it works

Every opening, straight from the source.

Wraith crawls Kenyan company career pages every night — banks, insurers, telcos, universities, counties — plus the applicant systems behind them (Workday, Oracle HCM, SmartRecruiters) and the major Kenyan job boards. A classifier throws out the noise, drops anything already posted or closed, and sorts the rest by field, so what lands in each group is an actual opening with an actual title and a link.

  • Career pages + job boards, checked nightly
  • Sorted by field: banking, tech, legal, HR, tax & audit, internships
  • New roles only — deduplicated, delivered from 06:30
